# Final Decision Framework

## Core decision logic

1. **Separate variant causality from molecular rescuability.**
2. Treat **p.Leu737Ter** as the established pathogenic loss-of-function allele.
3. Treat **N1002K** as a candidate missense variant with unresolved pathogenicity and unresolved phase.
4. Test rescue only as a falsifiable hypothesis.

## Route A — NAD+–SIRT2 stabilization

- **Lead:** Niacin
- **Mechanistic benchmark:** NMN
- **Backup:** Nicotinamide riboside (NR)

Goal: test whether supporting the NAD+–SIRT2 axis increases functional BUBR1 abundance.

## Route B — Proteostasis support

- **Lead:** Arimoclomol
- **Backups:** GGA/teprenone, bimoclomol, TUDCA

Goal: test whether proteostasis support improves the handling of a potentially unstable BUBR1 missense protein.

## Decision criterion

### GO
Advance only when:
- BUBR1 abundance/stability improves, **and**
- checkpoint/chromosome-segregation function improves.

### NO-GO
Do not advance when:
- no molecular rescue occurs,
- protein abundance rises without functional rescue,
- or chromosome-segregation phenotypes worsen.

## Interpretation boundary

This framework is for experimental prioritization. It is not evidence that niacin, arimoclomol, NMN, or any other candidate treats MVA or rescues N1002K in this patient.
