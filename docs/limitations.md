# Limitations

BUBR1-RESCUE 3.0 is a computational and literature-guided drug-repurposing framework. No original wet-lab rescue experiment was completed.

## Genomic limitations

- **p.Leu737Ter** is treated as the established pathogenic loss-of-function allele.
- **p.Asn1002Lys (N1002K)** remains a candidate missense variant.
- N1002K pathogenicity is **unresolved**.
- Phase between p.Leu737Ter and N1002K is **unresolved**.
- The project does **not** claim a confirmed compound-heterozygous molecular diagnosis from these two variants alone.

## Structural limitations

- Structural interpretation of N1002K is predictive.
- A high-confidence structural region does not prove that the substitution is pathogenic.
- No experimental protein stability, folding, abundance, or enzymatic measurements were obtained for N1002K.
- No experimentally measured ΔΔG is available.
- The project therefore describes N1002K as a **structure-supported rescue candidate**, not as a proven destabilizing variant.

## Drug-repurposing limitations

- No candidate has been shown here to rescue N1002K.
- Niacin has not been demonstrated to rescue BUBR1 N1002K.
- Arimoclomol has not been demonstrated to rescue BUBR1 N1002K.
- NMN is used as a mechanistic benchmark because of direct BUBR1 pathway evidence; this does not establish it as a treatment for MVA.
- Candidate scores are not clinical efficacy scores.

## Experimental limitations

The project did not complete its proposed laboratory validation.

Therefore, no claims are made regarding:
- increased BUBR1 abundance after treatment,
- restoration of spindle assembly checkpoint function,
- reduction in chromosome missegregation,
- reduction in aneuploidy,
- patient-specific response,
- dose, exposure, toxicity, or therapeutic benefit.

## Proposed future validation

A future Go/No-Go experiment should test whether a candidate:
1. increases BUBR1 abundance or stability,
2. restores checkpoint-related function,
3. reduces chromosome-missegregation phenotypes.

A **GO** decision requires both molecular and functional improvement.

A **NO-GO** decision applies when protein abundance increases without functional rescue, or when no meaningful rescue occurs.

## Interpretation principle

The central distinction in BUBR1-RESCUE 3.0 is:

**causality evidence ≠ rescuability evidence**

A variant may be worth testing for molecular rescue even when its pathogenicity is not yet established, provided uncertainty is made explicit and the experimental hypothesis is falsifiable.
