# BUBR1-RESCUE 3.0 — Evidence Strength Analysis

## Question

Which rescue strategy is better supported as the **primary hypothesis** for BUBR1-RESCUE 3.0?

- **Route A:** Niacin → NAD+ → SIRT2 → BUBR1 stabilization
- **Route B:** Arimoclomol → heat-shock/proteostasis response → BUBR1 stabilization

This analysis deliberately separates **direct evidence** from **inference**.

---

## Route A — Niacin / NAD+–SIRT2

### Step 1 — Niacin increases NAD+

**Evidence: DIRECT**

Hara et al. showed in human cells that nicotinic acid can increase cellular NAD+ through NAPRT-dependent metabolism.

### Step 2 — SIRT2 is NAD+-dependent

**Evidence: ESTABLISHED**

SIRT2 is an NAD+-dependent deacetylase.

### Step 3 — SIRT2 regulates BUBR1 abundance/stability

**Evidence: DIRECT FOR BUBR1**

North et al. showed that SIRT2 maintains BUBR1 in a deacetylated state and that SIRT2 overexpression can increase BubR1 abundance.

### Step 4 — NAD+ precursor support can increase BubR1

**Evidence: DIRECT FOR THE PATHWAY**

The same study showed that NMN treatment increased BubR1 abundance in vivo.

### Step 5 — Niacin increases BUBR1 in MVA or N1002K

**Evidence: ABSENT**

No direct evidence identified.

### Step 6 — Niacin restores checkpoint function in N1002K

**Evidence: ABSENT**

No direct evidence identified.

### Route A conclusion

The pathway has a **strong target-specific mechanistic bridge**:

**NAD+ / SIRT2 → BUBR1**

The main unresolved bridge is:

**Niacin → BUBR1 rescue in N1002K/MVA**

This makes niacin a defensible **primary repurposing hypothesis**, but not a demonstrated rescue treatment.

---

## Route B — Arimoclomol / proteostasis

### Step 1 — Arimoclomol enhances the heat-shock response

**Evidence: DIRECT**

Human-cell studies support enhanced HSP induction in stressed cells with arimoclomol.

### Step 2 — Some MVA-associated BUBR1 missense proteins depend on heat-shock machinery

**Evidence: DIRECT IN DISEASE-RELEVANT VARIANTS**

MVA-associated BUBR1 variants including I909T and L1012P showed reduced stability, and HSP90 inhibition markedly decreased mutant BUBR1 levels.

### Step 3 — Arimoclomol stabilizes BUBR1

**Evidence: ABSENT**

No direct BUBR1-specific evidence identified.

### Step 4 — Arimoclomol rescues N1002K

**Evidence: ABSENT**

No direct N1002K-specific evidence identified.

### Translational note

Arimoclomol is FDA-approved for neurological manifestations of Niemann-Pick disease type C in combination with miglustat. However, the FDA label states that the mechanism by which arimoclomol exerts its clinical effects in NPC is unknown.

### Route B conclusion

Arimoclomol has a **strong disease-mechanism rationale** if N1002K causes a folding/stability defect, but the bridge:

**Arimoclomol → BUBR1 stabilization**

remains inferential.

This makes arimoclomol best positioned as an **orthogonal rescue hypothesis**.

---

## Side-by-side stress test

| Question | Niacin / NAD+–SIRT2 | Arimoclomol / proteostasis |
|---|---|---|
| Direct evidence on BUBR1? | **Yes** — via SIRT2/NMN pathway | **No direct arimoclomol→BUBR1 evidence** |
| Evidence in MVA-associated BUBR1 missense variants? | Indirect | **Yes** — HSP90 dependence of unstable variants |
| Requires N1002K to be a stability defect? | Less strongly | **Strongly** |
| Human-use / translational knowledge? | Extensive for niacin | Strong; FDA-approved in NPC |
| Direct N1002K evidence? | No | No |
| Main inferential jump | Niacin → BUBR1 rescue | Arimoclomol → BUBR1 rescue |
| Best role in 3.0 | **Primary hypothesis** | **Orthogonal hypothesis** |

---

## Current decision

### Primary hypothesis
**Niacin / NAD+–SIRT2**

Reason: it has the more target-specific mechanistic chain because the NAD+–SIRT2 axis has direct experimental evidence linking it to BUBR1 abundance.

### Orthogonal hypothesis
**Arimoclomol / proteostasis**

Reason: it is highly relevant if N1002K proves to be a stability/misfolding defect, but the BUBR1-specific therapeutic bridge remains more inferential.

### Mechanistic benchmark
**NMN**

Reason: it is the intervention with the strongest direct experimental evidence for increasing BubR1 abundance through the NAD+/SIRT2 pathway.

---

## What would falsify the model?

### Niacin route — NO-GO if:
- NAD+ rises but BUBR1 does not,
- BUBR1 rises but checkpoint function does not improve,
- or chromosome missegregation is unchanged/worsened.

### Arimoclomol route — NO-GO if:
- N1002K does not produce a stability/proteostasis defect,
- HSP induction does not increase functional BUBR1,
- or increased BUBR1 fails to restore checkpoint function.

---

## Bottom line

BUBR1-RESCUE 3.0 is stronger when it presents **niacin as the primary testable repurposing hypothesis, NMN as the mechanistic benchmark, and arimoclomol as an independent proteostasis hypothesis**.

The value of the second submission is therefore not “more drugs,” but a clearer, evidence-graded, falsifiable framework showing exactly where the evidence is direct and where the project is still making an inference.
