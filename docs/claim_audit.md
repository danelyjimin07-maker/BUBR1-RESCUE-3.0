# BUBR1-RESCUE 3.0 — Claim Audit

## Purpose

This document defines which claims can be stated directly in the final report, which require cautious wording, and which should not be presented as conclusions.

---

## 1. SAFE CLAIMS

These statements are suitable for direct use in the report.

### Genomic / disease claims

- **BUB1B p.Leu737Ter is a pathogenic loss-of-function allele.**
- **BUBR1 dysfunction is associated with Mosaic Variegated Aneuploidy (MVA).**
- **N1002K is a rare BUB1B missense variant identified in the case analysis.**
- **N1002K lies in the C-terminal kinase-like/pseudokinase region of BUBR1.**
- **Phase between p.Leu737Ter and N1002K is unresolved.**
- **N1002K pathogenicity is unresolved.**
- **No confirmed compound-heterozygous diagnosis is claimed from these two variants alone.**

### Mechanistic claims

- **SIRT2 is an NAD+-dependent deacetylase.**
- **Experimental work has linked SIRT2 to BUBR1 stability/abundance.**
- **NMN has been reported to increase BubR1 abundance in vivo in an NAD+/SIRT2-linked context.**
- **Nicotinic acid can increase cellular NAD+ through NAPRT-dependent metabolism in human cells.**
- **Some MVA-associated BUBR1 missense variants have shown reduced protein abundance/stability.**
- **Some unstable BUBR1 missense variants show dependence on heat-shock machinery, including HSP90.**

### Project-result claims

- **Twenty candidate rescue strategies were scored using a transparent internal prioritization framework.**
- **Niacin ranked highest as the primary repurposing hypothesis in the project scoring system.**
- **NMN ranked as the strongest mechanistic benchmark.**
- **Arimoclomol ranked as the strongest orthogonal proteostasis hypothesis.**
- **The project uses explicit Go/No-Go criteria requiring both molecular and functional rescue.**
- **No original wet-lab rescue experiment was completed.**

---

## 2. QUALIFIED CLAIMS

These statements may be used only with cautious wording such as:

- *may*
- *could*
- *is consistent with*
- *supports the hypothesis that*
- *is mechanistically plausible*
- *warrants testing*

### N1002K structural interpretation

Use:

> N1002K **may alter** local electrostatics, packing, or hydrogen-bonding in the BUBR1 C-terminal region.

Do not use:

> N1002K destabilizes BUBR1.

---

Use:

> N1002K is **compatible with a full-length protein that may retain partial function or reduced stability**.

Do not use:

> N1002K produces a partially functional protein.

---

Use:

> The position and chemistry of N1002K, together with precedent from other MVA-associated BUBR1 missense variants, **support testing a stability/rescue hypothesis**.

Do not use:

> N1002K has the same mechanism as I909T or L1012P.

### Niacin hypothesis

Use:

> Niacin **could support** the NAD+–SIRT2–BUBR1 stabilization axis.

Do not use:

> Niacin stabilizes BUBR1 in MVA.

---

Use:

> Niacin is the **primary repurposing hypothesis** generated by the project.

Do not use:

> Niacin is the best treatment for this patient.

---

Use:

> The evidence chain **supports testing whether** niacin-mediated NAD+ support increases functional BUBR1.

Do not use:

> Niacin rescues BUBR1.

### Arimoclomol hypothesis

Use:

> Arimoclomol **provides a mechanistically plausible proteostasis strategy** if N1002K causes a folding/stability defect.

Do not use:

> Arimoclomol stabilizes N1002K.

---

Use:

> Arimoclomol is an **orthogonal rescue hypothesis**.

Do not use:

> Arimoclomol treats MVA.

### Functional outcome

Use:

> Increased functional BUBR1 **could improve** spindle assembly checkpoint function.

Do not use:

> Increasing BUBR1 will correct aneuploidy.

---

Use:

> A successful rescue strategy would be expected to reduce chromosome-missegregation phenotypes **if sufficient checkpoint function is restored**.

Do not use:

> The proposed treatment will reduce aneuploidy in the patient.

---

## 3. DO NOT CLAIM

The following statements should not appear as conclusions in the report, abstract, README, figures, or pitch.

- **N1002K is pathogenic.**
- **N1002K is the confirmed second disease-causing allele.**
- **p.Leu737Ter and N1002K are confirmed to be in trans.**
- **The patient has a confirmed compound-heterozygous BUB1B diagnosis based on these two variants.**
- **N1002K is experimentally unstable.**
- **N1002K is misfolded.**
- **Niacin rescues N1002K.**
- **Niacin treats MVA.**
- **NMN treats MVA.**
- **Arimoclomol rescues BUBR1.**
- **Arimoclomol rescues N1002K.**
- **4-PBA rescues BUBR1.**
- **The ranking predicts clinical efficacy.**
- **The highest-scoring compound is the best treatment.**
- **The project identified a cure.**
- **The project validated a therapy experimentally.**
- **Any dose or treatment regimen is recommended for the patient.**

---

## 4. Preferred language for the final report

Use wording like:

> “We prioritized...”

> “The evidence supports testing...”

> “This mechanism is biologically plausible...”

> “The variant remains of unresolved significance...”

> “The project proposes a falsifiable hypothesis...”

> “No direct evidence currently demonstrates...”

> “Further functional validation is required...”

Avoid wording like:

> “We proved...”

> “This drug works...”

> “This mutation causes...”

> “This will treat...”

> “This is the best therapy...”

---

## 5. Core final conclusion approved for Submission 2

> **BUBR1-RESCUE 3.0 prioritizes niacin as a testable NAD+–SIRT2 repurposing hypothesis, NMN as a mechanistic benchmark, and arimoclomol as an orthogonal proteostasis hypothesis. The framework does not claim therapeutic efficacy; instead, it defines a transparent, evidence-graded and falsifiable path for testing whether residual BUBR1 can be molecularly rescued.**

---

## 6. Abstract-safe version

> **BUBR1-RESCUE 3.0 is a genotype-guided drug-repurposing framework for BUB1B-associated MVA. It combines variant interpretation, structure-informed rescue reasoning, a reproducible multi-candidate screen, evidence-strength grading and Go/No-Go validation logic. Niacin emerged as the primary repurposing hypothesis through the NAD+–SIRT2–BUBR1 axis, while NMN serves as a mechanistic benchmark and arimoclomol provides an orthogonal proteostasis strategy. N1002K pathogenicity and phase remain unresolved, and no wet-lab rescue was demonstrated.**
